# Research Notes

This directory contains the research layer supporting the development of **Specification-Driven Operations (SDO)**.

The purpose of this area is to separate:

- the methodology's current normative documentation;
- the historical and conceptual origin of the idea;
- comparisons with adjacent disciplines;
- references used to validate terminology and originality claims.

## Planned research topics

- Spec-Driven Development (SDD) and specification-first engineering;
- Business Process Management (BPM/BPMN);
- workflow management systems;
- Design by Contract;
- data contracts and API contracts;
- requirements engineering;
- quality management systems;
- stage-gate models;
- Lean and built-in quality;
- IT service management;
- formal methods and executable specifications;
- human-in-the-loop and agentic workflows;
- auditability and provenance;
- exception management.

## Research rule

SDO should not claim novelty for concepts that already exist in established disciplines.

Research should instead identify:

1. what concepts SDO inherits or adapts;
2. what combination or operational framing may be distinctive;
3. where terminology conflicts with existing usage;
4. what claims can be supported by evidence;
5. what remains an untested hypothesis.

## Status

Research is incomplete in Concept Draft v0.1. No claim of academic novelty or exclusivity is made at this stage.
