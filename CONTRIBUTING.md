# Contributing to SDO

SDO — Specification-Driven Operations is an open methodology proposal.

The goal of this repository is not to defend a finished framework. It is to test whether the methodology is understandable, useful, adaptable, and robust across different organizational contexts.

Contributions are welcome, especially when they challenge assumptions or expose where the methodology does not work well.

## What contributions are useful

Useful contributions include:

- conceptual criticism;
- unclear or contradictory terminology;
- failed adoption attempts;
- real-world implementation experiences;
- alternative handoff patterns;
- exception-handling problems;
- examples from different industries or process types;
- mappings to existing systems and workflow tools;
- improvements to templates and examples;
- evidence that an SDO concept already exists elsewhere;
- research references relevant to the methodology.

Positive feedback is welcome, but evidence of failure or ambiguity is especially valuable because it helps improve the methodology.

## How to contribute

For small questions, criticism, or observations, open an Issue.

For documentation changes, examples, or proposed model changes, open a Pull Request.

When proposing a conceptual change, please explain:

1. the problem being addressed;
2. the current SDO concept affected;
3. the proposed change;
4. why the change improves applicability, clarity, or consistency;
5. any real-world evidence or references supporting it.

## Implementation reports

If you try SDO in practice, a useful report should include:

- the type of process tested;
- which SDO concepts were applied;
- which existing tools were used;
- what worked;
- what created friction;
- which concepts were unclear;
- which exceptions occurred;
- whether the handoff model was practical;
- what had to be adapted.

There is no requirement to disclose confidential company information.

## Adaptation is expected

SDO is intentionally implementation-independent.

Organizations may adapt terminology, tools, forms, system mappings, workflow states, and implementation details.

> **Adapt the implementation. Preserve the principles.**

If an adaptation appears to require changing a core principle, that is useful feedback and should be discussed openly.

## Research and prior art

SDO does not claim ownership of the underlying concepts of specifications, workflow validation, traceability, process management, evidence, exception handling, automation, or agentic execution.

If you know of research, standards, methodologies, or systems that overlap with SDO, please share them. Prior art helps clarify the methodology's actual contribution and prevents unsupported novelty claims.

## Licensing

Contributions to methodology documentation are understood to be submitted under the repository's **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)** license unless explicitly stated otherwise and accepted by the maintainers.

## Current status

SDO is a **Public Draft**, not a finished standard.

> **Try it. Challenge it. Adapt it. Share what worked and what did not.**
